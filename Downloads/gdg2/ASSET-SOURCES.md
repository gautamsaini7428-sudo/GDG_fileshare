# Visual asset source notes

- `client/public/assets/usar-campus.jpg` is the 720×480 campus exterior image used in the landing-page hero. It was selected as an actual USAR/GGSIPU campus photo, not generated artwork.
- The associated gallery is [USAR, GGSIPU campus photos](https://knwdle.com/colleges/university-school-of-automation-and-robotics-new-delhi/gallery). The page says its campus photos are sourced from official institute sites or Wikimedia Commons and that any Commons attribution is shown under the photo. The landing page links to the source gallery beside the image.
- The university-linked [East Delhi Campus inauguration album](https://photos.app.goo.gl/2NTw7cQdN5WtYj5M7) was also inspected; it is a 2023 GGSIPU/UITS event album and was not used as the hero asset.
- The custom FileShare page mark is an application identity, not a reproduction of the GGSIPU crest or university seal.
